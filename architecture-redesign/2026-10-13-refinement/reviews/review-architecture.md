## Good-spine checklist

- Real divergence points: exact observation/source identities, authoritative versus model evidence, target/label clocks, task promotion, ranking policy, learned-only verification, API permissions and external recovery are now owned and bounded.
- Enforceable rules: promotion requires matching live grants; labels cannot enter feature manifests; ranking requires one registered policy; exports require the immutable approval tuple and current base. These are concrete future validation obligations, not assertions that software already implements them.
- Inheritance: AD-1 through AD-8 and AD-10 through AD-12 retain their evidence, snapshot, safety and experimental meaning. AD-9's old weighted/percentile policy is intentionally replaced by v2 lexicographic policy and must be amended in the new spine, rather than silently inherited. AD-5 must include task-specific grants; AD-10 must retain analyzer-resolution semantics while naming the new learned-only outcome. The old package remains historical and unchanged.
- Brownfield fit: collector/identity/mask/recovery reuse is identified as reuse, not as a deployed API or pretrained stack. Lexical imports do not become resolved compiler edges. Current scope-changing module stock delta is preserved; fixed-origin-member targets need a new adapter. Imported domains retain approximation status and never become verified own observations.
- Capability coverage: numerical forecasting and pretrained detection/retrieval/remediation remain equally central research tracks. Both A and B precede primary-thesis selection. Missing fresh module history does not dismiss imported repository forecasting; plausible patches do not establish repair success. Static evidence preserves usability without replacing the AI thesis.
- Operational envelope: single-host metadata/artifacts, isolated runtimes, one worker, bounded host reservations, untrusted build phases, disk backpressure, fencing/quarantine, restore reconciliation and licensed retention are covered here or explicitly inherited. Multi-host scaling remains outside the initial study.
- Deferred precision: exact tier boundaries, classifier license resolution, task calibration, model/environment locks, capacity and scientific results are future prerequisites with registration/promotion gates. None silently authorizes normal deployment.
