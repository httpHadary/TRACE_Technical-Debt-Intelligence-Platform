## Decision: discover information before tuning another model

The strongest feasible next study is a matched, rights-cleared information ablation:
current-stock persistence → observed history → complete past activity/product
state → independently available directional intent. A compact signed distribution
centered on persistence remains a sensible candidate, but is not promoted.

Existing synthetic gains depend on engineered signal relationships. Unseen history
shapes did not test transport of those relationships. Confidence, familiar histories
and correct timestamps do not establish a stable future mapping. No real feature
importance or causal effect can be inferred from those experiments. The original
Java tests and previously exposed dataset families remain development/reference
material and cannot be relabeled a fresh confirmation set.

The new review adds task-specific evidence and practical availability constraints;
see [signal research](PHASE_6_6_PREDICTIVE_SIGNAL_RESEARCH.md) for primary sources,
reviewed sections and limitations. Refactoring/SATD, commit-level prediction,

estimands. Candidate utility must be tested against the actual30/60/90-day task.
