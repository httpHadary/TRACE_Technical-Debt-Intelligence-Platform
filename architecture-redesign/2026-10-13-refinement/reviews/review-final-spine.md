## Good-spine rubric

- **Real divergence points:** shared evidence/ID/clocks/domains, ports, owned state, capability, scientific selection, ranking, approval/export and external recovery are decided. UI implementation details and scale expansion remain deferred with no alternate evidence semantics permitted.
- **Enforceable prevention:** immutable manifests, exact selectors, task keys, registered RankPolicy, atomic grant/fencing checks, isolated credentials and approval tuples prevent the divergences named by their ADs. Future code must prove them; the design does not claim it already does.
- **Deferred items:** final tier thresholds, model licensing/loading, cohort precision, as-of admission and host fit have explicit registration/admission/abstention gates. Independent units cannot silently invent normal-product behavior while they are unresolved.
- **Technology floor:** fresh digests provide publisher contracts and checkpoint hashes for the new model roles. Existing Python 3.12.3, SQLite 3.45.1, td360-v3 0.15.2 and CatBoost 1.2.8 remain inspected brownfield seeds; FastAPI 0.142.2 has its primary release link. They are not a tested combined model environment. Package/runtime locks follow future loading checks. TTM's actual short branches and mean/quantile/class semantics are retained rather than guessed.
- **Brownfield and inherited safety:** current collector/identity/recovery concepts are reused without claiming the new API, HF adapters or research harness already exist. Imported history is never own VERIFIED history. Lexical graphs do not become resolved compiler evidence. Scope-changing module stock remains distinct from a new fixed-member target.
- **Operational/environmental dimensions:** one host/worker, private SQLite/artifacts, isolation, tenant/auth/research permissions, resource/disk admission, quarantine/fencing, restore reconciliation and licensed retention are covered by the spine and solution contract. Qwen's parameter payload is source-backed; 24 GB is now explicitly a study-host budget rather than a proven GPU minimum. Runtime/quantization/latency remain unmeasured.
- **User correction:** no remediation-first preference remains binding. Both early experiments govern primary selection; data/license blocks are distinct from measured model failure. A conventional forecast winner or valid negative study remains a possible outcome without disguising completion of pretrained research.
