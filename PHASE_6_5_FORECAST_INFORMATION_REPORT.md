## Finding: more information can help, but validity must transport

Earlier failures were not resolved by another history-only tuning grid. Phase6.4
already repaired the unsafe selector; its remaining reversal/hidden-event failures
show that history similarity and model confidence do not reveal an invisible change
in the conditional future relationship. The older real Java results remain exposed
reference evidence, not a new holdout or proof of a unique causal explanation.

This stage implemented a compact information-aware Adaptive Persistence model:
current stock + validation-admitted fraction of an empirical conditional signed-
change median, followed by nonnegative reconstruction. Direction probabilities,
raw signed deltas, cell support, unavailable-signal reasons and zero adjustments
remain visible. The joint distribution retains zero-change mass and both signs.
No future label, generator identity or partition is a predictor input.

The research question is about the information set. Compare debt-history sign,
unsigned past activity and an imperfect origin-known declared-intent record. Plans
are generated before random future realization, cancellation and direction reversal.
The signed record is a DELIBERATELY ENGINEERED synthetic information channel. Its
utility is assumed in the generator and then measured in the pipeline; it is not
evidence that actual plans, churn or Git history predict real debt.

Two adaptive policies use the same trained distributions and fixed shrink choices
0/.5/1. Local validation admits adjustments when its improvement/support/protection
criteria hold. Stress validation additionally includes paired hidden/reversed
future relationships. Locally selected shrink is1 at all9 seed/horizon cells;
stress-selected shrink is0 at all9. The latter is reliable abstention here, not
successful anticipation or a model that detects invisible drift online.

## What the independent control establishes

The same input request produces exactly the same prediction under different future
laws. The independent auditor verified34,560 equal forecast pairs. A model cannot
select different actions for unobservable worlds simply by increasing its size or
confidence threshold. For balanced identical-history endpoints c−a,c,c+a, ordinary
persistence minimizes total absolute loss; unit tests enumerate that restricted
example. This is not a proof that ALL real debt changes are unpredictable.

The scientifically useful result is conditional: extra origin-known directional
information CAN improve a persistence-centered predictor when its relationship is
stable, but neither a valid timestamp nor familiar history proves that stability.
The project's available admitted evidence does not establish which actual
software-development signals meet that condition. See the separate real-evidence
requirements and information audit for the needed prospective/family-held-out test.
