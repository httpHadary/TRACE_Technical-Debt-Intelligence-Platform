# A staged path to legitimate independent forecasting evidence

**DESIGN ONLY. No collection, admission, fitting, scoring or deployment is approved.**
All24 Phase C decisions remain NOT_APPROVED. Keep prior experiments and exposure
registries frozen. This document proposes a new study; it does not rewrite Phase5
or claim that the synthetic stages completed a real-data gate.

## First deliverable: an owner evidence package

Ask a qualified human reviewer and an authorized archive owner for a scoped review
package. Do not contact anyone or transfer data now. The package must identify:

- Exact source/data owners and permitted use: collection, retention, transformations,
  local model fitting/evaluation and each proposed publication/redistribution use.
  Confirm analyzer/output/derived-data permission separately from source-code terms.
- An explicit study mode: operational-as-of, or retrospective fixed-analyzer
  reconstruction. Later reconstruction is not evidence of historical online access.
- Immutable source/revision/family/branch identities and independently documented
  exposure/alias/fork relationships; original records and scope of the archive.
- Past feature records with event, record-version and actual availability clocks,
  original plan versions/edits where relevant, complete-window evidence and null
  reasons. Current ticket bodies/branch tips are insufficient historical proof.
- Native stock observations and source-state linkage at exact cutoffs/endpoints,
  analysis execution/availability clocks, target units and domain/rule/profile/scope
  manifests. No conversion from issue count, SATD, churn or actual labor to minutes.
- Allowed release metadata, retention/deletion policy, privacy treatment for author
  identities/text and exact documentary references. No synthetic signatures or
  approval inferred from public access or academic purpose.

Use the existing Phase C unsent clarification draft as input to human review. If
new permission is obtained, preserve the old NOT_APPROVED snapshot and record a
separately authorized versioned decision with genuine scoped proof and authority.
No approval setter or self-approved state is implemented in Phase6.6.

## Study design after admission

Construct exact endpoint stock, signed delta and endpoint direction for30/60/90
calendar days. Record any-event-within-horizon as a DIFFERENT estimand only when
event histories/censoring support it. An unchanged endpoint need not mean no
intermediate change. Preserve observed zeros, real timestamps and missingness.

Freeze origins/roles from metadata coverage before reading outcome magnitudes.
Maintain training, validation/model selection, calibration, and independent final
roles. Keep forks/shared lineages in one repository family. Use temporal AND
family holdouts, and a custodian-managed never-exposed final population.

For same-family temporal splits, purge complete input/target intervals. Reuse the
existing rule: earlier origin+h must be STRICTLY before later origin−90days.
A90-day target plus90-day context needs more than180days between boundary origins
under that strict rule, not a generic90-day embargo. Across different repositories,
prior-role labels still must actually be available before the pooled model's later
forecast/selection boundaries. Do not use later project observations merely because
the held-out repository differs. Freeze preprocessing, feature choices, point model
and calibration before final predictions; seal outputs before scoring labels.

Compare on identical eligible origins:

1. Persistence, with zero adjustment always available.
2. A small history-only signed-distribution or regularized delta control.
3. History + verified activity, and history + compatible product-state covariates.
4. Combined F0/F1; optional reconciled F2 only after its separate completeness gate.
5. Directional intent only after independent availability/permission evidence.

Use bounded, interpretable models first. Compare an integrated persistence-centered
zero/positive/negative distribution with direction/magnitude estimates and an
MAE-consistent median point action; retain raw signed adjustments and clipped stock
separately. Direction probability times conditional mean is not generally the
MAE-optimal median. A high F1 score alone does not establish a good stock forecast.
No pretrained model is necessary for initial signal discovery.

Feature-group removal and past-time-shift controls must test incremental utility.
A future-information feature is rejected, never included as an operational baseline.
Check domain resets, path conflicts, missingness/selection bias and plan cancellation.
A cancelled plan's cancellation is a feature only when available by that origin.

## Decision criteria to preregister using development evidence

The ultimate claim is joint positive skill at30/60/90 days, across new families and
later periods, while protecting unchanged cases and both large signs. Freeze a
practical gain threshold and harm budgets using development variability and user
costs before final outcomes. A5% macro-MAE gain is a candidate practical threshold
from the earlier design, not a new evidence-backed universal rule or guaranteed gain.

Primary comparisons: per-horizon repository-macro MAE, paired persistence skill,
equal-family/scale-sensitive summaries and raw error. Pooled metrics stay separate.
At zero baseline error, relative skill is undefined; report absolute errors/counts.
Account for all attempted origins and missing labels; do not condition comparisons
on favorable native successes or changing endpoints alone.

Report changed/unchanged, large increases/decreases, false/missed changes, calibration
and Brier/PR/ROC diagnostics where defined, inference failures, latency and memory.
Derive sample/cluster needs from a permitted development pilot; no invented universal
repository count or power guarantee. Repository-family paired intervals must respect
repeated origins and overlapping horizons. With too few independent families, report
exploratory precision, not a confirmatory victory. Joint superiority across three
horizons needs a preregistered simultaneous/multiplicity procedure; do not silently
apply the older90-day-primary protocol to a new three-horizon claim.

Calibrate80% risk intervals with independent calibration data and the SAME feature-
availability/fallback policy used at inference. Register width/interval-score limits
with coverage, including missing-signal strata. Phase6.5 exposed fallback/radius
mismatch; do not fix it on frozen test residuals. Delayed risk monitoring can update
only after exact-horizon labels mature, become available and retain the same domain.
It cannot promise to anticipate the first unobservable distribution shift.

## Outcomes and boundaries

If activity improves risk but not the stock median, retain persistence for point
forecasts and evaluate risk utility separately. If directional signals improve only
familiar projects, do not promote them. If no information survives independent
validation, report the negative result and preserve persistence. If rights or
provenance cannot be established, stop at evidence design. No synthetic score can
substitute for this evidence, and no next phase starts automatically.
