# Evidence needed to make future debt changes predictable

No real source, signal or target is admitted by this document. All24 Phase C
permission decisions remain NOT_APPROVED. No new analyzer data was accessed.

## Smallest defensible future study

The promising next experiment is an information ablation on independently admitted
longitudinal observations, not a larger model. Compare the SAME origins and labels:
current-stock persistence → past debt behavior → past development activity →
versioned directional/intent information. Keep an uncertainty/risk prediction even
when the absolute-loss median remains persistence. An activity signal can identify
volatility while being unable to identify the sign of a future stock change.

| Information | Required origin evidence | Useful hypothesis | Main failure/leakage risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Debt history | Immutable compatible analysis, stock units, source revision, observed/available times, null reasons | Recent direction, plateau and change frequency may persist | Carried states mistaken for independent observations; scope/rule reset |
| Development activity | Full past reachable commit lineage, first-parent/dedup/path policy, complete-window proof, availability evidence | Event probability or magnitude changes with recent activity | Today's tip/history mistaken for knowledge at the old cutoff; future churn |
| Directional source/process signals | Past semantic source categories or versioned intent declarations, exact as-of snapshots, field-level permission | Some kinds of origin-known work anticipate debt addition/removal | Completed refactorings, merged outcomes and final ticket text reveal the future |
| Declared plans | Original creation plus every revision/cancellation actually available at origin | Planned remediation/growth can alter conditional direction/magnitude | Plans fail or reverse; a deadline is not proof of realized changes |
| Measurement domain | Pinned analyzer/rules/profiles/production scope, bytecode/source pairing and unit definition | Avoid apparent debt changes caused by measurement changes | Treating analyzer drift as software deterioration/improvement |
| Delayed feedback | Actual target measurement and its availability time, horizon-specific pending-origin queue | Monitor sign/magnitude error and recalibrate only after labels arrive | Using a90-day outcome to update a model at day30; claiming to detect the first invisible shift |

Legitimacy and predictive utility are independent gates. A timestamp-valid field can
be useless or unstable, and a predictive field can be unauthorized or leaked. Public
access, pseudonymization and a forecasting model's license resolve neither question.
Author identifiers and issue text add privacy/redistribution questions; prefer
minimal admitted aggregates where scientifically adequate. No legal approval is
created here, and the original rights dossier remains authoritative.

## Required contract and target semantics

Use the existing Phase5 source/domain and dual-clock contracts as the basis. Each
record needs immutable source identity, event time, availability time, scope, unit,
completeness and missing reason. Read the historical version available by origin,
not the final issue/PR/plan state or a nearest later measurement. A retrospective
fixed-analyzer reconstruction has different availability assumptions from online
forecasting and must be labeled accordingly.

Current stock and exact origin+h stock define ENDPOINT change. The stock can change
and return to its starting value before dayh. Therefore P(endpoint differs) is not
P(any event beforeh). Sparse endpoint observations cannot label continuous stable
periods. Study event hazards separately only when event/censoring evidence exists.
The primary estimand remains native estimated maintainability remediation minutes,
not actual developer labor, issue count, code churn or model confidence.

## Independent validation and uncertainty

1. Obtain documentary rights for exact fields, transformations, local learning,
   retention and intended release. Separately authorize collection/runtime use.
2. Audit a small development sample for as-of reconstruction, source/target domain
   stability, missingness and changed/unchanged/sign support. No new data is read now.
3. Preregister feature families and a small persistence-centered distribution model.
   Train preprocessing and magnitude distributions only on development training.
4. Use development repository-family/temporal/mechanism holdouts for feature choice
   and risk tolerances. Purge overlapping horizon windows (at least90days where
   origins cross role boundaries), and group forks/shared lineages appropriately.
5. Reserve previously unexposed repositories AND later time periods. Do not tune on
   old Java cohorts or the exposed synthetic populations. Origins with missing
   targets remain in eligibility accounting, not fabricated unchanged labels.
6. Estimate sample needs from development-only paired-error variability and cluster
   structure; require informative stable/up/down strata at every horizon. Do not
   invent a universal minimum repository count or claim synthetic-case independence.
7. Seal prediction requests and outputs before independent scoring. Use repository-
   macro and pooled metrics separately; report normalized and raw skill, sign and
   unchanged errors, failures, width/coverage/interval score, latency and memory.
8. Test feature-removal and time-lag controls and negative controls. Stable process
   associations must survive family/temporal holdouts. Conditional-shift monitoring
   is evaluated with realistic label delays and a frozen update rule, not hindsight.

If no directional information survives these tests, retain persistence for point
forecasts and evaluate risk distributions for decision support. If permissions or
measurement consistency remain unavailable, stop at contracts and evidence design;
do not substitute restricted labels or another analyzer's incomparable metric.

## Recommended architecture, pending evidence

Persistence is always the protected action. A compact hurdle/mixture distribution
represents zero, positive and negative endpoint change with a conditional magnitude
law. Use its conditional median for MAE-centered point actions; use direction
probabilities and intervals for risk. Probability times conditional mean is not an
MAE-optimal median in general. Apply bounded validation-admitted shrinkage and
abstention when signal coverage/provenance/support are inadequate. Preserve raw
signed changes separately from nonnegative stock reconstruction.

The new implementation is deliberately an interpretable conditional empirical
model, not an unvalidated assertion that plans drive real debt. Its stress-aware
selector represents uncertainty about the signal relationship explicitly. A robust
choice of persistence does not establish anticipation; a local positive score does
not establish transport. No model promotion is authorized by a synthetic gain.
