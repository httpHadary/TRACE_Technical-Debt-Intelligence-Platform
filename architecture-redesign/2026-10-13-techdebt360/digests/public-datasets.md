# Public datasets: exact 90-day forecasting versus detection and remediation

Research date and access date for **every source below: 2026-10-13**. Scope: the seven named resources, their public asset metadata, bounded file previews, and associated primary publication records. No installation, repository scanning, training, experiments, or large dataset downloads were performed. A 64 KiB HTTP range of the replication ZIP was sufficient to inspect its central directory. “Verified” below means the stated documentation/metadata/sample was retrieved, not that a complete dataset has passed a quality audit.

## Decision

**None of these seven resources establishes reliable, ready-to-use, exact 90-calendar-day ground truth for total technical debt or realized engineering labor.** There are genuine numerical forecasting resources, so it would also be incorrect to say these are all detection datasets. The strongest immediately inspectable numerical series is Zenodo 4534110; NPF supplies file-level future labels, but its `TD_3m` is **13 weeks = 91 days**. The Technical Debt Dataset provides commit-level measurements from which a calendar-cutoff target could potentially be reconstructed. Tesoro and the repayment dataset serve substantially different detection/prioritization questions. These are decision inferences from the evidence below, not empirical evaluations of predictive performance. [NPF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/blob/main/README.md), [forecasting data](https://zenodo.org/records/4534110), [TDD repository](https://github.com/clowee/The-Technical-Debt-Dataset), [Tesoro schema](https://github.com/NamCyan/tesoro#data-structure), [repayment CSV](https://github.com/yikun-li/satd-repayment-effort/blob/main/satd_repayment.csv).

## Resource findings

### 1. The Technical Debt Dataset

Verified: current README distinguishes v1.01 (32 projects, all branches, **SonarQube timeline problems**) from v2 (31 projects, master only), warns about duplicate issue observations, and licenses data **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. Release 2.0.1 was published **2021-11-04**, with the large SQLite asset above; no database download/schema execution occurred. [Maintainer README](https://github.com/clowee/The-Technical-Debt-Dataset), [release metadata](https://api.github.com/repos/clowee/The-Technical-Debt-Dataset/releases).

The original **ACM PROMISE paper, 2019-09-18**, describes a different historical version: 33 Apache Java projects, 78K commits, >200 days of collection. Its documented schema has `SONAR_MEASURES(projectID, commitHash, SQAnalysisDate, …)`, `SONAR_ISSUES(creationCommitHash, closeCommitHash, creationDate, …)`, project URLs, Git data, Jira, SZZ and refactoring records. SonarQube 7.5 produced rule-based remediation estimates. Do not mix paper counts with v2 coverage, or use analysis execution dates as Git dates. [Author-hosted ACM paper, §§3, 6 and Figure 2](https://www.taibi.it/sites/default/files/2019%20-%20Promise%20-%20The%20Technical%20Debt%20Dataset%20-%20ACM%20Version.pdf).

Inference: v2 is a promising reconstruction input, not a certified forecast mart. A later audit must establish commit-time joins, master coverage, scan completeness, and metric semantics before extracting `state at t0 + 90 days`. [Version and timeline warnings](https://github.com/clowee/The-Technical-Debt-Dataset#how-to-use-it).

### 2. NPFDataSet

Verified publication: **MaramAllah/Hugging Face, uploaded 2026-09-15**, license **unknown**. The card claims six repositories (OFBiz, SystemML/SystemDS, Groovy, NiFi, Guava, OkHttp), weekly scans from 2015-01-01 for 60 weeks, and a custom pipeline; it cites no associated peer-reviewed paper or public pipeline repository. `TD_1m` means four weeks; `TD_3m` means thirteen. TD includes active bug/vulnerability/code-smell remediation minutes, which differs from treating only maintainability/code-smell debt as the target. [Dataset API](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet), [card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/blob/main/README.md).

The bounded CSV preview verified `repository_id`, `artifact_id`, `lineage_id`, `t0`, `commit_hash_t0`, analyzer/profile versions, eligibility, future effort components, statuses, timestamp-audit columns and partitions. It also contains non-Java/ineligible rows and `UNKNOWN` labels. First sampled rows use `sonarqube_version=26.8.0.126808`, `analysis_mode=SOURCE_ONLY`, while `t0` is in 2015: these are retrospective modern-analyzer measurements, not evidence of scans performed in 2015. [Actual CSV](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/resolve/main/combined_td_mart.csv).

The card admits heuristic lineage and unimplemented identity counters. It recommends observed-label filtering and purged chronological splits. Those are useful controls, not proof they work. Viewer metadata exposes approximately 250K rows and four repository classes, versus six claimed in the card; full coverage and usable-label counts remain unresolved. Also unresolved: weekly scan time versus `t0`, which the card defines as historical commit time, actual horizon timestamps, deletion semantics, and absence of future-derived features. [Card limitations](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/blob/main/README.md), [viewer/schema](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet).

### 3. Forecaster toolbox

Verified: SDK4ED forecasting backend, **MIT** repository license; `forecaster_service.py`, `model_training.py`, `utils.py`, environment and test files exist. It forecasts remediation minutes and also energy/security, with bundled `data/*_measures.csv` examples. It is principally a service/code resource, not a separately established measurement corpus. [Maintainer repository](https://github.com/XAI-toolkit/forecaster-toolbox), [tree inventory](https://api.github.com/repos/XAI-toolkit/forecaster-toolbox/git/trees/master?recursive=1).

Source inspection found future targets constructed with `.shift(-intermediate_horizon)`, version counters, chronological train/test splitting, and `TimeSeriesSplit` without an explicit horizon purge in the inspected calls. **Inference:** horizon means observation steps; exact calendar-day interpretation and overlapping-label safeguards require an adapter/audit. A week-indexed horizon of 13 is 91 days, while irregular samples have variable elapsed time. No models were executed; historical dependencies were not compatibility-tested. [Model source](https://github.com/XAI-toolkit/forecaster-toolbox/blob/master/model_training.py).

### 4. Zenodo 6109615

Verified: **Silva, Vergilio and Farah / Zenodo, 2022-02-16**, metadata license **CC BY 4.0**, resource type “Other.” ZIP central directory includes `original datasets/apache_groovy_measures.csv`, the other original project CSVs, `scripts/training-testing/main_original_study.py`, configuration scripts, result CSVs/PDF charts, and `spreadsheets/mape-parameters-configurations.xlsx`. The record explicitly says it reuses Tsoukalas et al.’s dataset/design and adds LSTM. [Record/API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/6109615), [ZIP source](https://zenodo.org/records/6109615/files/replication%20package.zip?download=1).

Inference: useful independent-author **method replication**, not independently collected ground truth. Public metadata does not establish a peer-reviewed venue for this exact replication title; searched publisher metadata did not locate a matching final publication. Contents were inventoried, not validated or executed. Upstream data have different license metadata; the package’s CC BY tag does not itself resolve the original dataset’s reuse terms. [Replication record](https://zenodo.org/records/6109615), [original record license](https://zenodo.org/api/records/4534110).

### 5. Zenodo 4534110

Verified: **Tsoukalas, Kehagias, Siavvas and Chatzigeorgiou / Zenodo, 2020-08-08**, v1.0, **CC BY-NC-ND 2.0**. Includes `_benchmark_repository_measures.csv`, 15 OSS project CSVs and `company_projectA_measures.csv`/`company_projectB_measures.csv`. Examples cover Groovy, Dubbo, Kafka, NiFi, OFBiz, SystemML, Commons IO, Guava, Openfire, Java-WebSocket, Jenkins, Spring Boot, OkHttp, Retrofit and ZXing. [Original metadata/file inventory](https://zenodo.org/api/records/4534110).

Direct CSV inspection: semicolon delimiter; `date`, `sqale_index`, `normalized_td`, remediation components, smells, size and complexity metrics. Groovy has **150 rows, 2015-12-25–2018-11-02, seven-day gaps**. Project A has **62 rows, 2018-12-21–2020-03-04, gaps from 1 to 85 days**. The 209-row feature-selection benchmark has `project_name` and static metrics, **no `date`**. Hence the benchmark is not a longitudinal forecasting target table, and calendar frequency must be verified separately per project. [Groovy CSV](https://zenodo.org/records/4534110/files/apache_groovy_measures.csv?download=1), [Project A CSV](https://zenodo.org/records/4534110/files/company_projectA_measures.csv?download=1), [benchmark CSV](https://zenodo.org/records/4534110/files/_benchmark_repository_measures.csv?download=1).

Associated peer-reviewed article: **Elsevier, Journal of Systems and Software 170, December 2020**, DOI `10.1016/j.jss.2020.110777`; publisher-deposited metadata confirms journal-article status. Exact 90-day measurements, actual labor costs and broad repository transfer remain unproven. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jss.2020.110777), [publication metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.jss.2020.110777).

### 6. Tesoro

Verified files: `data/tesoro/tesoro_code.json` (~6 MB), `tesoro_comment.json` (~93 MB), detector and fold JSONs; linked Hugging Face `tesoro-code` export has **1,255 examples**, Parquet-backed data. Code schema: `id`, `original_code`, `code_wo_comment`, `cleancode`, `repo`, four binary labels for DESIGN/IMPLEMENTATION/DEFECT/TEST. Comment schema adds comment text/context and classification including DOCUMENTATION/NONSATD. Published license **MIT**. No timestamp or numerical effort column occurs in these documented schemas. [Repository/schema](https://github.com/NamCyan/tesoro#data-structure), [asset inventory](https://api.github.com/repos/NamCyan/tesoro/git/trees/main?recursive=1), [HF schema](https://huggingface.co/datasets/NamCyan/tesoro-code/blob/main/README.md).

Provenance: comments/code from **974 Java projects**, SATD-derived classification; therefore debt-type recognition is not an independently measured whole-codebase debt amount. Associated **ACM TOSEM journal article, 2026-03-13**, final title *Detection of Technical Debt in Java Source Code*, DOI `10.1145/3801745`. The arXiv v2 page still says under review; newer publisher-deposited metadata resolves that stale status. [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05457), [ACM publication metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1145/3801745).

Inference: strongest of this set for code/comment detection, with project-held-out, clone-grouped evaluation needed to assess transfer. It does not provide exact-90-day targets. Repository MIT labeling alone is not a completed audit of every upstream code snippet’s obligations. [Schemas and provenance](https://github.com/NamCyan/tesoro), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.05457).

### 7. SATD repayment effort

Verified: **Yikun Li, Mohamed Soliman and Paris Avgeriou / GitHub**, **MIT**, single CSV. Actual header is `Message,LCC,MCC,HCC,CCC,LA,LD,FA,FM,FD,SATD`; no repository ID, commit hash or timestamp is exported in this header. README claims **341,740 SATD items from 2,568,728 commits across 1,060 Apache repositories**; these are author-reported corpus counts, not a full-file count performed here. [Repository](https://github.com/yikun-li/satd-repayment-effort), [actual CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yikun-li/satd-repayment-effort/main/satd_repayment.csv).

The associated manuscript defines repayment via lines/files changed and four AST-change significance counts; these are **effort proxies, not hours/minutes or an observed future debt stock**. It identifies repayment-related SATD in commit messages, creating a practical deployment mismatch if one wants to predict before the fix message exists. Current title is *Text Tells the Cost: Predicting and Analyzing Repayment Effort of Self-Admitted Technical Debt*, originally submitted **2023-09-12**, latest arXiv v3 **2026-04-26**. Matching final peer-reviewed publication was not verified; label as preprint in this digest. [Primary manuscript §§2.3–2.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.06020v3), [version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06020).

Inference: valuable remediation ranking research, but chronological/cross-repository leakage tests cannot be reconstructed from the exported CSV alone; seek mapping metadata rather than inventing it. [Exported schema](https://github.com/yikun-li/satd-repayment-effort/blob/main/satd_repayment.csv).

## Backfill feasibility and evaluation conditions

These are **planning recommendations/inferences**, not measured results or authorized implementation steps:

- Define the contract before collecting: exact **90 × 24 hours** (or explicitly specified calendar/timezone convention), file/class/repository aggregation, absolute stock versus change, and named analyzer metric. Preserve source commit and cutoff timestamps separately from analysis execution time. The examined resources mix per-file all-issue minutes, project SQALE metrics and categorical/change proxies. [NPF target definition](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/blob/main/README.md), [original CSV schema](https://zenodo.org/records/4534110/files/apache_groovy_measures.csv?download=1), [TDD schema](https://www.taibi.it/sites/default/files/2019%20-%20Promise%20-%20The%20Technical%20Debt%20Dataset%20-%20ACM%20Version.pdf).
- Weekly interpolation, nearest-date substitution, or carrying a weekly observation to day 90 creates an **estimated label**, not a measured day-90 label. For exact cutoff reconstruction, select the repository state at the cutoff (last reachable commit at or before it) and analyze that state using a fixed analyzer/profile. Commit-level historical measurements may avoid repeat scans if their coverage and provenance are sound; this has not been established for v2. [TDD history/version caveats](https://github.com/clowee/The-Technical-Debt-Dataset), [weekly versus irregular examples](https://zenodo.org/records/4534110).
- Freeze analyzer/rules/profile/language coverage; otherwise changing detection rules can look like changing debt. Record rename/copy/deletion semantics and distinguish missing observations from zero debt. NPF’s version/profile fields are useful, but its heuristic lineage and missing-target statuses need auditing. [NPF card and sample](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet).
- Split **by time and repository**, group clones/lineages, fit transforms/feature selection only on training periods, and purge origins whose future-label interval crosses the validation/test boundary. Exclude every future path, future effort component, label/status and post-origin event from features. NPF’s metadata makes an audit possible; the toolbox’s plain time-series split does not on its own establish a horizon purge. [NPF split guidance](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/blob/main/README.md), [toolbox model source](https://github.com/XAI-toolkit/forecaster-toolbox/blob/master/model_training.py).
- Forecast at each repository’s actual frequency; do not equate “13 samples” with 90 days. Report exact-day evaluation separately from week-ahead replication. A 60-week corpus with 180-day trailing features and 91-day future labels has substantially less usable origin history than its raw length suggests, even before purge/missingness. [NPF scan/window contract](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/blob/main/README.md), [Project A dates](https://zenodo.org/records/4534110/files/company_projectA_measures.csv?download=1).

**Cost scenario, not benchmark:** six repositories × 60 weekly snapshots is **360 scan jobs**. At an assumed 10–60 minutes/job this is **60–360 compute-hours**; daily snapshots over 420 days would be 2,520 jobs, **420–2,520 compute-hours**. These exclude checkout, compilation/dependency repair, retry, storage, server ingestion, annotation and licensing effort; fixed cutoff pairs may require fewer jobs than daily scanning. Formula: jobs × assumed minutes ÷ 60. The 2019 TDD authors’ >200-day historical collection is evidence that backfill can be expensive, **not a current runtime estimate**. No current costs were measured, and its old concurrency-license explanation was not checked against current SonarQube editions. [Historical collection paper §11](https://www.taibi.it/sites/default/files/2019%20-%20Promise%20-%20The%20Technical%20Debt%20Dataset%20-%20ACM%20Version.pdf), [NPF weekly design](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaramAllah/NPFDataSet/blob/main/README.md).

## What would change this decision

Obtain/audit an exact-cutoff scan manifest and complete usable-target counts, reconcile NPF’s six claimed versus four preview repositories, secure its license/provenance, and validate temporal/lineage semantics. For numerical forecasting, retain Zenodo 4534110 as a historical baseline and consider TDD v2 reconstruction; treat 6109615 as replication, not extra independent observations. For detection/remediation, Tesoro and the repayment corpus supply more directly matching labels, while neither warrants promises of reduced labor or exact-90-day debt prediction. No load-bearing accuracy comparison, current deployment compatibility claim, or independent replication of exact-90-day performance was established in this research.
