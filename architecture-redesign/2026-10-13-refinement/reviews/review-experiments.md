The repaired document requires A/B evidence before primary selection, keeps horizon and domain contracts distinct, and separates task-specific outcomes instead of combining MAE, F1 and repair rates. The B3 first-candidate and any-of-three endpoints use units rather than candidate attempts as denominators. Human acceptance neither overrides missing checks nor follows automatically from passing tests.

No remaining scientific claim of valid labels, independent sample size, statistical power or model superiority was found beyond explicitly proposed gates. Runtime/API assertions are preserved as strategy-owned planning contracts; this review did not execute them or certify a working harness.
