**PASS — synthetic engineering experiment only.** The pinned model genuinely
consumed both90 and512 observed daily positions on the same36 eligible cases.
Longer context worsened30-day MAE and improved60/90-day MAE in this designed
population. This does **not** establish real technical-debt forecasting gains,
a generally better context length or a new production default. Persistence remains
the mandatory real-study reference. No original benchmark was rerun or retuned.
